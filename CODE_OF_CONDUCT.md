# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful community. Treat people with
kindness, assume good intent, and give constructive feedback about ideas and
work rather than individuals.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and publication of
private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when behavior conflicts with these expectations. Report concerns privately
through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/glossia/glossia/security/advisories/new).
