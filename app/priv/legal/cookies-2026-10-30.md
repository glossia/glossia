Cookies and similar browser storage support sessions, security and requested preferences. This policy covers the hosted service at glossia.ai.

## Browser storage

### Session cookie: `_glossia_key`

Holds session state, including authentication, security and temporary workflow state. It is a session cookie, with no persistent expiry configured. Browser session restoration can affect its lifetime.

### Language preference: `glossia_locale`

Remembers an explicitly selected interface language for up to 365 days.

### Drafts: browser `sessionStorage`

Preserves unsent conversation and guidance text during reloads and reconnects. It uses browser-session storage; saved drafts are cleared by the relevant workflow.

The CSRF protection token is held within the session. It is not a separate `_csrf_token` cookie.

## Analytics and external services

The site also loads analytics and external resources described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy), including analytics served from `analytics.glossia.ai`, Google Fonts and, on relevant pages or when enabled, Gravatar, Sentry and Cloudflare Turnstile. Loading an external resource discloses connection information independently of whether it sets a cookie. We do not claim that every external resource is cookie-free.
