## Data and purposes

Depending on your use, we process:

- **Connection and security information:** IP addresses, request information and browser details, to deliver and protect the service.
- **Inquiries:** email addresses and correspondence, to answer questions and arrange onboarding. The interest form emails the address you submit to the operator.
- **Accounts and integrations:** profile details, language preferences, organization membership, provider identifiers and authorization credentials, to authenticate users and manage access.
- **Content and collaboration:** project and repository information, source content, translation instructions, outputs and conversation messages, to provide the workflows you request.
- **Usage and diagnostics:** event information and error reports, to understand service operation, troubleshoot problems and analyze use.

Information necessary for a requested feature is required to use that feature. Avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in content or instructions.

## Analytics and external resources

The website includes analytics served from `analytics.glossia.ai`. Configured service-event analytics can include account and user identifiers, event names, timestamps and resource identifiers. These records are not necessarily anonymous.

Glossia's localization-analytics feature processes page and referrer information, language, device and country information. It derives a daily, project-scoped visitor identifier from an IP address and User-Agent rather than storing those two inputs in the analytics event row. Depending on configuration, country lookup sends an IP address to `ipapi.is`, with `country.is` as fallback. A website using this feature is responsible for its own notice and any required consent.

Stylesheets request Google Fonts. Some signed-in pages request Gravatar images using a digest of the account email; that digest is not anonymous. Where enabled, Sentry receives error diagnostics and Cloudflare Turnstile processes browser challenges and verification tokens. These requests disclose connection information to the relevant service. Browser storage is described in the [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

## Legal bases

Processing necessary to provide requested services or take pre-contractual steps at your request relies on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. This includes onboarding inquiries and requested account, translation and integration workflows where that basis applies.

Necessary service security, troubleshooting and operational administration rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, subject to balancing your rights and interests. Our interests are protecting accounts and infrastructure and maintaining a functioning service. Processing required by law relies on Article 6(1)(c). Where consent is required for optional processing or device access, it must be obtained separately and may be withdrawn. This notice does not itself constitute consent.

## Retention and security

Retention depends on the record and its purpose. Account, project and conversation records support ongoing use; correspondence supports the inquiry and any resulting relationship; diagnostic and security records support investigating faults and abuse. Legal retention obligations, resolving claims and backup rotation may require different handling from active records. Contact us to request account closure or deletion and to discuss the records and retention requirements applicable to your use.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Access to connected services depends on the credentials and permissions you provide; review those permissions and revoke integrations you no longer need.
