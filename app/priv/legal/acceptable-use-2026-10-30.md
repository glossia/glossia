## Scope and contact

This policy applies to the hosted Glossia service operated by **Pedro Piñera Buendía**, Jessnerstrasse 27a, 10247 Berlin, Germany. Questions and abuse reports: [pedro@glossia.ai](mailto:pedro@glossia.ai).

## Prohibited conduct

Do not use the service to:

- Commit or facilitate unlawful activity, fraud, phishing, malicious impersonation or exploitation of children.
- Threaten people, facilitate violence or targeted harassment, or unlawfully expose private information.
- Infringe intellectual-property, confidentiality, privacy or other legal rights, including by submitting content or credentials without authorization.
- Distribute malware or spam, compromise accounts or systems, or interfere with the service or other users.
- Bypass access controls, impersonate another account, evade proportionate enforcement, or deliberately defeat documented usage limits.
- Obtain unauthorized access to another person's repositories, content, credentials or data.

These rules concern harmful conduct and unlawful use. They do not prohibit lawful translation, documentation, reporting or analysis of sensitive subject matter merely because the source discusses it. Security testing requires authorization unless otherwise permitted by law.

## Reports and enforcement

We may investigate credible reports and take proportionate action, including restricting access or removing unlawful content. Immediate action may be necessary to protect people or systems or comply with law. Where appropriate and lawful, we will give reasons and an opportunity to respond. Contact us to report abuse or question a restriction. Disclosures to authorities remain subject to applicable law and the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
